\section{Status report}

\subsection{Proposal}\label{proposal}

\subsubsection{Motivation}\label{motivation}

\emph{{Learning Analytics has the potential to overhaul how students navigate their educational path. From early warning systems to modification of course content and personalisation of learning, in this dissertation, I will undertake exploratory avenues of research on various prototypes consisting of different implementations of Learning Analytics. Prior research done on Learning Analytics is vast, and yet the opinions of students are often relegated to the wayside. This study will offer unique perspectives from the student body. }}

\subsubsection{Aims}\label{aims}

\emph{{This project will explore prior issues with Learning Analytics projects with a thorough literature review focusing on four key topics; Ethics and law, Goals, Development, and Implementation. An initial round of interviews with various members of the student body will be then conducted to explore the student's knowledge of potential issues as well as requirements through the use of a group MOSCOW framework. An iterative and adaptive process will be used for the creation of prototypes which will then be merited by the student body. }}
